# MossBlocks

A crate-pushing puzzle game. Push every crate onto a glowing moss pit to clear the level.

- 100 levels in 5 worlds, getting harder as you go
- Every level is solvable
- Undo as many moves as you like; Reset is at the top
- Controls: D-pad, swipe, or tap a spot to walk there (arrow keys, Z to undo, R to reset on a computer)
- Progress saves on your device

Stuck? Open `guide.html` for hints and step-by-step solutions for every level.
