# Slither

A retro snake game on a little old-style phone. Eat apples to grow and clear each level.

- 10 levels, each with its own walls, getting faster as you go
- Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game
- Catch the blinking bug for bonus points
- Controls: swipe, or keypad 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 (arrow keys or WASD on a computer); 5 to pause
- Best score and unlocked levels save on your device
